A few useful answers

Frequently asked
questions.

The basics about sharing expenses with EvenUp.

What is EvenUp?⌄

EvenUp helps friends, households, and trip groups record shared expenses, split costs fairly, and keep track of who owes whom.

How do I split an expense?⌄

Add an expense to a group, choose who paid, and select the participants and split method that fit. EvenUp keeps each person's share clear.

Do my group members need an account?⌄

People who use the workspace should create an EvenUp account and join the group so they can see its expenses and balances.

Can I use EvenUp for trips and household bills?⌄

Yes. Create a separate group for a trip, shared home, dinner, or any other plan with shared costs.
